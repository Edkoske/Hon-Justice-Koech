About Justice Koech

A son of Soliat Ward, Justice Koech was born and raised in this community. His deep roots and understanding of local challenges drive his commitment to serve.

Education

First Class Honours in Geospatial Sciences from Egerton University. Professional Geographer committed to evidence-based leadership.

Community Leadership

Over 12 years serving in various community organizations, youth groups, and grassroots initiatives across Soliat Ward.

Why I'm Running

To bring accountable, transparent leadership. To ensure every voice is heard. To deliver tangible results for Soliat Ward families.

Understanding Soliat Ward

Our Ward

Soliat Ward is a vibrant community with rich agricultural potential, diverse population, and strong cultural heritage. Our ward comprises several villages with hardworking families who deserve better services and opportunities.

Key Challenges We Must Address

Poor Road Network

Many areas remain inaccessible, especially during rainy seasons, affecting trade and emergency services.

Limited Electricity

Several villages still lack reliable electricity, hindering economic activities and student learning.

Water Scarcity

Clean water access remains a challenge for many households, affecting health and productivity.

Our Vision for Soliat Ward

A thriving, connected community where every resident has access to quality education, healthcare, clean water, and economic opportunities. Where infrastructure supports growth, and leadership serves with integrity.
